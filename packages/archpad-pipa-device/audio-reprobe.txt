ArchPad audio reprobe service
=============================

Linux 7.1.4 on Xiaomi Pad 6 can leave the LPASS VA macro and dependent audio
components deferred after an early probe-order failure. If the Xiaomi Pad 6
ALSA card is absent, this bounded one-shot service reloads only
snd_soc_lpass_va_macro, waits for udev, and retries at most three times.

The service exits immediately without changing modules when the ALSA card is
already present. This is a temporary device-integration measure; the long-term
fix is to correct the kernel probe dependency/order and then remove the
service.
